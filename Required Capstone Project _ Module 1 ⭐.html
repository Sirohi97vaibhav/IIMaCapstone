Learning Outcomes Addressed

  • Understand how AI, GenAI, and Agentic AI work and where they apply in business.
  • Use prompts effectively for analysis, decision-making, and content generation.
  • Map business workflows and identify where AI can drive automation and efficiency.
  • Evaluate AI opportunities using feasibility, ROI, and impact criteria.
  • Build AI adoption roadmaps aligned to business goals and KPIs.
  • Apply data governance, privacy, and ethical AI principles.

Phase 1 – Problem Framing & Generative AI Foundations

Milestone 1: AI Opportunity Brief

Estimated Effort: 1 Hour

Word Limit: 1000 words

Objective

In this first capstone milestone, you will identify a real business workflow that could potentially benefit from Generative and Agentic AI.

You will prepare a short AI Opportunity Brief that explains:

  • the workflow problem

  • why AI may be useful

  • the potential business value

This brief establishes the foundation for your entire capstone project, so choosing the right workflow is important.

In later modules, you will progressively expand this idea into a complete AI transformation proposal.

Note: This is a required assignment and counts towards programme completion.


Task Overview

Complete the following three steps.

Step 1 — Select a Business Domain

Choose one organisational function where a workflow could benefit from AI support.

Examples include:

  • Human Resources – recruitment screening, policy queries, onboarding documentation

  • Finance – invoice processing, expense audits, financial reporting

  • Sales – proposal drafting, lead qualification, CRM updates

  • Supply Chain – supplier communication, shipment documentation, demand reports

  • Customer Support – ticket triage, response drafting, knowledge base search

  • Operations – internal reporting, process documentation, compliance tracking

You may also choose another relevant domain if it contains a clearly defined workflow.

Step 2 — Identify a Workflow Problem

Select one specific operational workflow within the chosen domain.

A workflow is a series of steps used to complete a business task.

Examples:

  • reviewing supplier invoices

  • drafting sales proposals

  • responding to support tickets

  • analysing operational reports

The workflow should contain multiple steps and coordination activities, not just a single action.

Step 3 — Write the AI Opportunity Brief

Document your response in the template provided below explaining how Generative AI could potentially improve the workflow.

The goal is not to design a full AI system yet.
Instead, focus on problem clarity and potential value.

Required Sections

Your AI Opportunity Brief must include the following sections.

1. Business Context

Briefly describe:

  • the organisation or department

  • the business function involved

  • the importance of this workflow

Example:

The finance department processes approximately 1,500 vendor invoices each month. The process involves manual verification, approval routing, and reconciliation with purchase orders.

2. Current Workflow Summary

Describe the existing workflow process.

Include:

  • the major steps involved

  • who performs them

  • any current tools or systems used

A simple step list is sufficient.

Example:

  1. Vendor submits invoice

  2. Finance team reviews invoice details

  3. Data is manually entered into accounting software

  4. Managers approve payments

  5. Finance reconciles invoices with purchase orders

3. Workflow Challenges

Explain the key operational issues.

Common problems include:

  • manual data entry

  • slow approvals

  • inconsistent documentation

  • repetitive analysis

  • difficulty retrieving information

Example:

Manual data entry creates processing delays and occasional errors. Approval cycles also slow down the payment process.

4. Why Generative AI Is Relevant

Explain how Generative AI could assist this workflow.

Consider capabilities such as:

  • document summarisation

  • information extraction

  • report generation

  • conversational querying of data

  • drafting communications

Example:

Generative AI could extract invoice data from documents, summarise key details, and generate approval summaries for managers.

5. Is Agentic AI Required?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can autonomously coordinate multiple steps in a workflow.

In this section, briefly state whether:

  • simple AI assistance is sufficient, or

  • an AI agent may eventually coordinate parts of the workflow.

Example:

In the long term, an AI agent could automatically process invoices, flag anomalies, and route approvals based on predefined rules.

6. Type of Business Impact

Identify the primary value the solution could create.

Possible impact categories include:

  • Efficiency – reducing time spent on repetitive work

  • Revenue enablement – improving sales productivity

  • Risk reduction – reducing compliance or operational errors

  • Decision support – improving analysis and insight generation

Example:

The main impact would be efficiency gains and reduced manual processing time.

7. Initial Value Hypothesis

Provide a simple estimate of how AI could improve the process.

This does not need to be precise.

Example:

If AI could automate data extraction and initial review, the finance team could reduce invoice processing time by approximately 40–50%.

Practical Tips

The quality of your capstone depends heavily on choosing the right workflow.

Choose Workflows That:

✔ Contain 5–10 steps
✔ Occur frequently in the organisation
✔ Involve repetitive information processing
✔ Allow estimation of time, cost, or errors

Avoid Workflows That Are:

❌ Too broad
Example: “Improve customer experience”

❌ Too narrow
Example: “Write an email reply”

❌ Purely strategic
Example: “Develop AI strategy”

Focus on specific operational workflows.

Example Scenario

Workflow Problem

A finance team manually processes vendor invoices.

Current Issues

  • manual data entry

  • slow approval cycles

  • occasional reconciliation errors

Potential AI Support

Generative AI could:

  • extract invoice data automatically

  • generate validation summaries

  • assist in detecting anomalies or inconsistencies

  • draft approval notes for finance managers


Submission

✔ Business context
✔ Workflow steps
✔ Key operational challenges
✔ Why Generative AI is relevant
✔ Whether Agentic AI may be needed
✔ Type of business impact
✔ Initial value hypothesis
